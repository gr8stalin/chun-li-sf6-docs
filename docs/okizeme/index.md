You know, offense and stuff.

Since she lacks a throw loop entirely and doesn't really have the same ambiguity in her moveset as other characters, Chun benefits from the "throw until they start teching" approach on oki more than others. 

Something to keep in mind is that while her tick throw potential is strong, singular strikes from Chun on an opponent's block aren't too threatening. Despite 5MP being +1 on block, it doesn't create much in the way of ambiguous spacing and the reward of being able to press 5LP isn't terribly strong without prior conditioning.
